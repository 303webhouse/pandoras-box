A mark's age, and when it is stale or wide

DRAFT for R-IV.938(e) · 2026-10-12 · CC-ABACUS

SAMPLE DATA, AND NOT SHIPPED. Every ticker, price and age here is invented; the page makes no API calls and runs no JavaScript. It needs two fields the hub does not serve yet — mark_as_of and mark_integrity (R-IV.935(d)) — so nothing on the live Agora has changed and nothing will until they exist.

1 · Why the age goes on the row at all

The Book row shows a P&L, and the panel shows a mark. Both are computed from the same price, so when that price is a day old, both figures are a day old — and today neither says so. A number with no age reads as current, which is the one thing it must not do when it isn't. So the age is shown in every state, not only the bad ones: an age that appears only when something is wrong teaches you to read its absence as "fine", and then a missing field looks like good news.

A price whose age is unknown is not the same as no price, and neither is the same as a stale one. The first says the hub could not tell us when; the second says there is nothing to show. Today the page has one phrase for the whole family, and a reader cannot tell which happened.

5 · The choices in this draft, for the principal to overrule

The figure is struck through, not hidden. A mark that cannot be trusted still produced the number in the book, and that number is the only clue to how far off the book might be. Hiding it would be tidier and would lose that. The strike is what says "do not read this as your position's worth".

Amber, not red. Amber already means "we cannot confirm this" everywhere else on Agora. An old or illiquid price is exactly that; it is not a fired breaker, and the two must not look alike.

The age is always shown. If it appeared only when something was wrong, its absence would come to mean "fine" — and then a field that stopped arriving would look like good news.

6 · What I need from BUILD before this can ship

1. Is mark_as_of an instant or a day? If it arrives as midnight UTC it is a DAY someone wrote down, and an "age" computed from it is wrong by up to a day — the trap R-IV.726 found in entry_date. An age needs a real instant.

2. What shape is mark_integrity? This draft assumes a flag that says the quote is wide. If it is a number (a spread width, a ratio), the page should not pick the threshold that decides "wide" — that is a judgement with one author, and it belongs with whoever measures the quote.

3. Does 26 hours come from the hub or from the page? The same answer: if the hub decides staleness, the page should render the hub's verdict rather than recompute it from a timestamp and risk the two disagreeing. If the page decides, 26 hours lives in one named constant.
