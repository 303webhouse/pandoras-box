2 · APIS and KODIAK upgrade a signal — they do not replace it

The card keeps its strategy’s name, shows the tag, and its outline brightens. APIS is a bull tag so it takes the lime family; KODIAK is bear, so vermilion — the palette’s existing meanings, not new colours.

SPINE’s recommendation, for you to judge: pulse only while the signal is fresh (the first 15 minutes), then hold the bright outline steady. Constant motion across several cards pulls attention away from everything else on the screen — including the cards that are not upgraded. Both states are below, side by side, so you can see the difference.

3 · A shadow strategy stays quieter than everything else

Shadow keeps its dashed outline, its shadow tag and no glow, so an upgraded card never has to compete with an unbacktested one for attention. An upgrade tag on a shadow card is not drawn here, because a shadow signal is never sized — if that combination can occur, it needs its own ruling before it gets a treatment.

5 · With “reduce motion” turned on, nothing pulses

The bright outline alone still says “upgraded”, so the only thing lost is the movement. If you want to see it: iPhone → Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion, then reload this page. The two FRESH cards above will hold steady and look exactly like the OLDER pair.
